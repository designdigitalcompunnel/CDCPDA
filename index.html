About CDCPDA

Rooted in the Central District.

Meet the McKinney Center →
Established in 2019

The Central District Community Preservation & Development Authority (CDCPDA) is a Washington State-chartered public organization working to preserve and strengthen Seattle’s historic Central District.

Learn About CDCPDA →
Community connection

Our Mission

Preserve cultural heritage

Protect the neighborhood’s Black cultural heritage and support community-led development.

Our Vision

More local opportunity

Build on the Central District’s legacy through education, enterprise, and room to gather.

Our Approach

Steward a shared space

Make the McKinney Center a place for local organizations, public programs, and community connection.
